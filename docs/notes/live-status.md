# Live status on route pages (on `main` since 31 Oct 2026; switched ON in Vercel at 16:06 UTC the same day)

## What exists
- `api/live.js`: root Vercel Function (Node CJS, like `api/scribe-token.js`). `GET /api/live/?from=<site station name>&to=<site station name>` returns the long-distance trains (ICE IC EC ECE RJ RJX TGV FLX) leaving A for B in the next 2 h: planned departure, delay at A, platform (and whether it changed), cancellation, and the delay or cancellation at B. Source: DB Timetables API, `/plan/<eva>/<yyMMdd>/<hh>` for the schedule and `/fchg/<eva>` for changes, joined on the trip id (the id minus its trailing `-<stop index>`). The route comes from `ppth` (planned path) of the departure: B must be in it.
- Switches: `LIVE_ENABLED=1`, `DB_CLIENT_ID`, `DB_API_KEY` in Vercel env (Production). Off or missing = 200 `{ok:false,error:"disabled"}` and the page shows NO live block (nothing to fix in preview deploys). The key never leaves the function.
- Protection against the 60 calls/min Free plan: CDN `s-maxage=45` (no stale-while-revalidate: the browser would refetch in the background, doubled requests and 90 s old data), per-instance caches (plan hour 20 min, changes 45 s), a 40-calls-per-minute ceiling answering `busy`, 429 from DB pauses calls for 30 s. Only stations in `data/live-stations.json` are accepted (no open proxy).
- `data/live-stations.json` (76 stations of the route pages -> EVA + DB's own spelling) from `scripts/live_stations.py` (about 5 min, 1.1 s between calls; writes only if every station resolves). DB spells some stations differently (`Münster(Westf)Hbf`, `Frankfurt(Main)Hbf`, `Frankfurt(M) Flughafen Fernbf`, `Freiburg(Breisgau) Hbf`, `Halle(Saale)Hbf`, `Hamm(Westf)Hbf`, `Minden(Westf)`, `Köln Messe`, `Siegburg`, `Singen(Hohentwiel)`): the `PATTERN` table in the script holds them; /station/<pattern> answers 404, not an empty list, for unknown names, and plain "Frankfurt Hbf" finds only the S-Bahn level (8098105).
- `components/LiveBoard.tsx` (client island above "Next-to-time" on `RoutePage`): fetches once, refreshes every 60 s while the tab is visible, keeps the last good answer when a refresh fails, renders nothing until the function answers. Strings `lib/dict/{en,de}/live.ts` (`live.*`, `footer.live`); credits line on How we measure.

## Single trains and saved trains (on main)
- `GET /api/live/?train=ICE%201541`: looks the train up in `data/live-trains.json` (`scripts/live_trains.py`, ~1 min: per train number over the last 2 months its most common origin+destination pair, mode of planned times, weekday bitmask, median minutes; only stations in live-stations.json, joined on the site's short name, minus the NO-LONG list). Then `/plan` of the origin for the departure hour finds the trip (match `tl c=` type AND `n=` number), `/fchg` at both ends gives delays; destination planned arrival from its plan hour (±20 min near hour edges). Window: 3 h before departure to 90 min after arrival, today's or yesterday's start, weekday bit must be set. Answer `{run:null}` outside the window.
- Building the table: group by origin+destination and take the mode of times; grouping by exact times drops half the trains because planned times shift by a few minutes between timetable versions. Use the same run key as the other scripts (`regexp_extract(id, '^(.*-\d{10})-\d+$', 1)`), not `train_line_ride_id`; inside an f-string the `{10}` must be doubled.
- NO-LONG station ids: some ids in live-stations.json answer `/plan` with only S-Bahn/regional trains (Düsseldorf Flughafen, Köln Messe/Deutz, Stendal Hbf, Wolfsburg Hbf). Probe every station id for long-distance types over three hours before trusting it ((local probe script, not kept), 1.1 s between calls) and keep the exclusion list in both `live_trains.py` and `lib/liveChange.ts`.
- Coverage: ~840 trains (about half of searchable long-distance trains, 34 of 50 train pages); rare, irregular and foreign-origin trains have no lookup, so the box simply doesn't show.
- Gaps in DB's feed: a destination plan hour can be empty (Berlin Gesundbrunnen late evening), and an arriving train can be missing from a station's plan hour; show no line rather than a guess.
- UI: `components/LiveNow.tsx` (`useLiveRun`, refresh 60 s while visible, renders nothing until found), `SaveButton` + `lib/saved.ts` (localStorage `gp-saved-v1`, max 8, `gp:saved` event), `YourTrains` on home (max 4 live lookups per visit to protect the 60 calls/min budget).
- Change trains live (`?at=<station>&a=<train>&ta=<min>[&b=<train>&tb=<min>]`, `lib/liveChange.ts`): A's live arrival at the change station, B's live departure, spare minutes; answers only when A's planned arrival is 1 h ago..4 h ahead. "Days like today" uses per-pair bins of A's arrival delay (`LATE_BINS` in connections.py and liveChange.ts must match), widened to neighbours until >= 8 days, at most 3 bins in all (a 4-bin widening printed "16–60 min", which no rider reads as "like today"). UI and data rules: `change-trains.md` (Live section).

## Data facts learned
- Local time stamps `yyMMddHHmm`. Berlin Hbf is EVA 8098160 (the level with ICEs); 8011160 returns an empty plan. A cancelled stop has `cs="c"` on `<dp>`/`<ar>`; a new platform is `cp`; `hi="1"` marks a hidden entry (skip it).
- A train's change record exists only once DB has a prediction: many trains an hour ahead have none, so "no delay reported" is the honest wording, never "on time". The arrival delay at B appears only when B has a change record for that trip.
- The planned arrival at B comes from B's `/plan` for the hour of the predicted arrival and the two before it.
- Real data check (31 Oct, 11:55 UTC): Köln to Berlin showed FLX 1325 75 min late at departure and 54 min late on arrival; all returned in 0.2 s cold, 3 ms cached.
- DB's "Timetables" licence is CC BY 4.0: attribution (Deutsche Bahn AG, link, licence) sits in the block and on How we measure. The WBT terms page it points to did not answer from the VPS.
